Environment Administration

Purpose​

You operate one knowledge environment on a multi-tenant platform. This area of the documentation tells you what you configure, where each control lives, and how to verify changes reach the surfaces you expect. Read this if you are onboarding a new environment, revising a configuration after researcher feedback, or auditing what your environment is doing.

The platform ships generic. Your job is to shape four things: how the platform speaks, who can do what, what data comes in, and what the platform records about how it is being used.

Features​

Ordered by how often a typical administrator uses each.

  • Voice and vocabulary substitution — the words the platform uses across every researcher-facing surface. Maturity: Active. Limit: scoped to platform-emitted wording; never rewrites researcher-authored content. See Voice and Vocabulary.
  • Permissions panel — contributors, role composition, project rules, ownership transfers, and the audit trail. Maturity: Active. Limit: the permissions that make up each standard role are fixed; custom roles compose from the same building blocks. See Permissions Panel.
  • Data import wizard — guided review of import proposals from Imports & AI → Imports, across Excel (.xlsx), MySQL, CSV bundle, and Coda sources. The platform parses your source first and asks AI for help second. Maturity: Active. See Data Import Wizard.
  • Workspace configuration — table labels, display fields, source-table scoping, default visible columns, and the saved views researchers share. Maturity: Active. Limit: after a large cold import, review each table's default view before handing it to researchers, because field definitions can still be broader than one source table. See Workspace configuration.
  • Vocabulary grounding — link your environment's vocabulary to external authorities such as Wikidata and PeriodO. Maturity: Active. Limit: only the tenant administrator role can confirm a grounding. See Vocabulary Grounding.
  • AI-assisted vocabulary bootstrap — a one-pass onboarding workflow that proposes an initial vocabulary set for your review, so the platform speaks your domain's language sooner. Maturity: Built. Limit: the proposal review screen is not yet available, so the workflow cannot be run end-to-end today; until it ships, vocabulary entries are authored directly on the live vocabulary surface. See AI-Assisted Bootstrap.
  • Efficiency tips — short contextual nudges shown to researchers in the import wizard and workspace; switch them on or off per environment and review aggregate acceptance numbers. Maturity: Active. Limit: tips never block a researcher's action; aggregate numbers are only reported when enough activity exists to keep individual researchers unidentifiable. See Efficiency Tips.
  • Citation integrity — structural guarantees behind citable editions and stable artifact versions. Maturity: Active. Limit: the platform does not mint DOIs itself; it exposes the stable identity an external DOI minter relies on. See Citation Integrity.
  • Media review surface — a single page to review everything the platform extracted from one version of a media artifact, with manual additions, a re-extraction dry run, and promotion to a citable edition. Maturity: Active. Limit: promoting an edition requires the administrator role. See Media Review Surface.
  • Entry Surface readiness — a review panel for your public search and browsing controls before you launch them. Maturity: Active. Limit: a review surface, not an auto-apply surface; making a control public is a separate, explicit step. See Entry Surface Readiness.
  • Telemetry and privacy posture — what the platform records, what it does not, where the data lives, how long it is kept, and the data-protection rights it supports. Maturity: Active. Limit: capture is off by default; enabling it is an explicit, recorded decision. See Telemetry.
  • Standards and citations — what the platform's standards support gives your environment without configuration, what you can configure on top, and what exports produce. Maturity: Built. Limit: some export profiles are still maturing; the standards overview carries per-item maturity. See Standards and Citations and the standards overview.
  • User and access management — an older overview of the standard roles; the Permissions Panel is the live source of truth. Maturity: Built (legacy summary). Limit: role composition and custom-role authoring live in the Permissions Panel. See User and Access Management.

Usability — how you use it​

The most common administrator paths:

  • Onboarding a new environment: configure voice and vocabulary by authoring entries directly on the vocabulary surface; confirm a few groundings if external authorities apply to your domain; compose permission roles and invite contributors; choose your citation style and have your platform operator apply it — a dedicated configuration screen is not yet available (see Standards and Citations); decide whether efficiency tips are on.
  • Bringing in new source data: open Imports & AI → Imports and use the upload button for your source type (Excel, MySQL, CSV bundle, or Coda). The wizard parses the source, classifies risk, generates proposals, and routes each proposal through a two-step review: a scholar approves it, then an administrator applies it.
  • Curating the workspace after import: open the workspace tables the import created, set or confirm their labels and display fields, hide irrelevant columns in the default view, and check which view is marked as default before inviting researchers in.
  • Reviewing media attestations: open the record from the workspace Media view and use the detail pane's Mesh→term tab — approve, reassign, or strip each part's term binding — and its Version tab to re-extract or pin a revision as a citable Edition (see Media Review Surface).
  • Auditing recent activity: open the Permissions Panel at /admin/permissions/ and use the Audit tab. Permission changes, ownership transfers, vocabulary edits, and reviewed AI-assisted changes all appear there.
  • Checking what the platform is recording: /admin/monitoring/telemetry/ shows your environment's telemetry summary — which signals are being captured and in what volume.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
The wording on a researcher-facing surface is still the platform defaultNo vocabulary entry exists for that wording, or the entry has not been confirmedOpen the vocabulary surface (see Voice and Vocabulary), find the matching key, and add or confirm an entry. Reload the surface to confirm the new wording.
The Permissions Panel shows a banner of vocabulary findingsThe vocabulary check that runs when the panel loads found missing entries, broken grounding references, or inconsistenciesClick into the surfaced rows. The check is read-only — acting on the findings is your decision.
An import proposal is marked as parser-only because AI was unavailableAI assistance is not configured, or not reachable, in your platform deploymentParser-only proposals are still fully reviewable. To enable AI assistance, contact your platform operator.
A re-import overwrote a researcher's manual editsThe run used full refresh, which reloads rows from scratch, instead of edit-preserving refreshRestore the affected records from your environment's backup, then re-run as an edit-preserving refresh. If your platform operator runs imports for you, ask them to use the edit-preserving behaviour.

If a failure mode is well documented in a specific feature page, that page owns the deeper troubleshooting; this overview only flags the top-level cases.
