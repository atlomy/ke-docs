AI-Assisted Vocabulary Bootstrap

Purpose​

A new environment opens with the platform's standard wording: the labels that title records in the workspace, and the confirmation phrases researchers approve before high-impact operations. Researchers are not blocked while that wording is still generic — the platform adapts its standard phrasing using the labels from your environment's own configuration.

The AI-assisted bootstrap shortens the path to wording that speaks your researchers' domain language. It generates a starter set of wording proposals tuned to the research discipline your environment declares and its concept families (the kinds of records your environment defines), for you to review — accept, reject, or edit — in one pass during onboarding.

Bootstrap is a one-pass onboarding tool. After onboarding, Voice and Vocabulary describes how your environment's wording resolves and the paths for shaping it. Note the availability limit below: the in-product screen for reviewing bootstrap proposals is not yet available, so the workflow cannot be run end-to-end today.

Features​

  • Proposal generation — Maturity: Built. Generates a starter list of suggested wording entries from your declared research discipline and concept families: suggested titles for records of each concept family, plus suggested phrasings for the four confirmation prompts researchers approve before high-impact operations (reorganising entries into a connected structure, permanently removing duplicate entries, updating the same identifier across already-saved entries, and applying a batch of structural changes as one step). Each proposal carries the suggested wording, a short explanation of why it is proposed, and a confidence score. Proposals come from built-in rules today; model-generated proposals are not yet available.
  • All-or-nothing commit — Maturity: Built. Accepted proposals are written to your environment's vocabulary in a single transaction. If any write fails, nothing is written; you can resolve the cause and retry without partial results to clean up.
  • AI-reviewed status with provenance — Maturity: Built. Every committed entry is recorded with AI-reviewed status and carries a tamper-evident cryptographic reference back to the proposal that produced it, so an entry's bootstrap origin stays provable. An explicit wording override for the same phrase takes precedence — explicit wording always wins over AI-reviewed wording.
  • Proposal review screen — Maturity: Deferred. The screen where you accept, reject, or edit each proposal is not yet available in the environment admin interface. Until it ships, your environment's wording is shaped through the paths described in Voice and Vocabulary — naming your concept families, renaming roles, and asking your platform operator about a specific phrase.

What you can do today​

Because the proposal review screen is not yet available:

  • Name your concept families well — the highest-leverage wording move available today. The platform substitutes those labels into its standard phrases automatically, so a good set of names improves many phrases at once. See Voice and Vocabulary for where to set them and for the other wording paths.
  • Rely on the standard wording in the meantime: record titles and confirmation prompts render from the platform's standard templates, adapted with your environment's own labels where available. An incomplete vocabulary does not block your researchers.
  • Review the vocabulary arriving in your data through Vocabulary Grounding — a separate surface for the values found in your imported records, not for the platform's wording.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
You cannot find a bootstrap or proposal-review option in your environment's admin interfaceThe proposal review screen is not yet availableShape your environment's wording through the paths in Voice and Vocabulary. If you expected bootstrap to be enabled for your environment, contact your platform operator.
Confirmation prompts show standard platform wording rather than your domain's termsNo environment-specific wording exists for those prompts, so the standard templates render with your labels substitutedThis does not block researchers. Check that your concept-family labels are set — the prompts substitute them automatically. For phrasing beyond that, contact your platform operator; see Voice and Vocabulary.
An entry's wording carries AI-reviewed status and you want different wordingAI-reviewed entries keep their wording until an explicit override replaces itAn explicit override takes precedence over an AI-reviewed entry, but there is no self-service screen for editing an individual wording entry today — contact your platform operator.
