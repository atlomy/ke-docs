Entry Surface Readiness

Purpose​

Before your environment exposes a public Entry surface — the published layer external readers will see — the readiness panel lets you review the filter ("facet") and autocomplete controls that would become public, record a decision about each one, and confirm or defer each candidate. Read this when preparing for a public launch, when a readiness report flags a compact-control warning, or when activating an additional public role.

The panel is a review surface, not an apply surface. Recording a decision stores review state for your environment; promoting a public control or confirming your environment's search configuration is a separate, explicit step performed with your platform operator.

Features​

  • Readiness report — for every active public Entry role in your environment, the report lists the reviewed filter and autocomplete fields, candidate fields, compact-control warnings, blockers, and all stored decisions, along with how many records were sampled to produce it. Maturity: Active. Viewing the report requires admin access in your environment.
  • Durable review decisions — each decision is stored with its action, your reason (required, up to 500 characters), your username, the time, and a snapshot of the evidence the report showed you. Maturity: Active. A decision is a review checkpoint; it does not change the public surface itself.
  • Fixed decision actions — review candidate before promotion; choose shorter public label; defer candidate with reason; defer public control with reason; acknowledge long-value exclusion. The action list is fixed; the reason is free text. Maturity: Active.
  • Environment isolation — the report and your decisions are always scoped to the environment you are signed in to. You cannot read another environment's report or record decisions for it. Maturity: Active.
  • Refreshed report on every decision — recording a decision returns the updated report, including the newly stored decision, and confirms that your environment's search configuration was untouched. Maturity: Active.

Usability — how you use it​

Sign in with an account that has admin access. The Entry Surface readiness panel currently lives in the transitional standalone shell at /app/admin/; daily operations and configuration tasks remain in the Django admin area at /admin/. The readiness panel sits in the side column, next to the source-data refresh panel, and loads the current report as the page opens. The refresh button at the top of the panel re-runs the report at any time.

The summary line shows the overall status — "Ready for scalar probe" once at least one public role has reviewed single-value filter fields, "Needs review" otherwise — along with the number of roles, warnings, and recorded decisions, and a "ratification unchanged" indicator confirming that review work never alters your environment's search configuration.

  • Review compact-control warnings. The report flags fields where some values are too long to show in a compact public filter control. Each flagged field appears with a reason box and the actions that are valid for it; the panel shows up to three fields per role at a time. A field you have decided on stays in the list, now showing its stored decision with the action and reason you recorded.
  • Record a deferral. When a candidate should not become public yet, defer it with a reason. The deferral is durable: the next report shows the stored decision, and the field stays deferred until you record a different decision.
  • Walk the pre-launch checklist. Each role section lists its blockers, reviewed fields, candidates, and recorded decisions. Work through them row by row; every decision requires a reason.
  • Hand off for launch. When the report reads clean and your environment is ready, your platform operator performs the public launch. Recording decisions is the review checkpoint that precedes the launch — it is not the launch itself.

If the server rejects a decision — a missing reason, or an action that is not valid for that field — the panel shows the server's message inline.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
The panel reports that readiness is unavailable, or you land on the sign-in pageYour account does not have admin access in this environmentAsk an existing environment administrator to grant admin access, or run the review from an admin account.
Recording a decision fails with a validation messageThe reason is missing, or the chosen action is not valid for that fieldRead the message in the panel. Add a reason, or choose one of the actions the panel offers for that field.
The panel reports no active public Entry rolesYour environment does not have a public Entry role configured yetPublic Entry roles are set up as part of preparing your environment for launch. Contact your platform operator.
The report reads clean but the public Entry surface has not appearedRecording decisions does not promote public controls or launch the surfaceConfirm with your platform operator that the launch step was run. The panel is a review surface only.
The "ratification unchanged" indicator worried youThis is by design — recording decisions never changes your environment's search configurationConfirming the search configuration is a separate explicit operation performed with your platform operator. The indicator exists so you can verify nothing changed as a side effect of your review.
You need to undo a recorded decisionDecisions are durable review stateRecord a new decision for the same field. It replaces the earlier one; the report then shows the latest decision with its reason and time.
