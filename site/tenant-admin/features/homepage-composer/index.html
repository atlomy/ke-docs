Homepage Composer

Purpose​

Your environment's public homepage is the first thing a reader sees. The homepage composer is where you decide what goes on it and in what order — a board of showcase blocks that draw on your own published content.

You arrange the board here; readers see the result on the public homepage. The board is read live: when you save, the public homepage reflects your changes on its next load. There is no separate publish step for the board itself — the composer is the board.

What a block can put in front of readers is still governed by the platform's publishing rules. A block only shows content that is published and current: an item you have not published, or a version that is no longer the current one, does not appear to readers even if it sits in the block. Arranging the board and publishing the underlying content are two separate decisions.

Features​

  • Compose the board — Maturity: Built. Add blocks with Add a widget, order them with Move up / Move down, and take one off the homepage with Remove widget. The board is a single ordered list — the order you set here is the order readers see.
  • A block for each kind of content, at its own readiness — Maturity: Built (catalog); readiness varies per block. The Add a widget picker groups blocks into Suggested for you (ready to add), More blocks (coming soon), and Already on your homepage (already placed). Each block shows where it draws from (for example, From your Media, From your Place data) and its readiness:
    • Building now — fully working: you can add it and choose exactly which items it shows. The 3D model showcase is at this stage — it draws on your 3D-model media and renders on the public homepage.
    • Scaffolded — you can place it and it explains in the board what it will draw on, but it does not yet take a hand-picked item list. The Map, Timeline, and Relationship network blocks are at this stage.
    • Design-ahead — shown in the picker so you can see what is coming, but not yet addable. The Image, Audio, and Video showcases are at this stage.
  • Choose and order the items in a block — Maturity: Built. For a block that is Building now, open its item picker — Choose items on an empty block, Manage items once it has some — a single grid of your media, shown by thumbnail so you pick visual items by sight. Clicking a thumbnail selects it; each selected item shows a number marking its reading order, and Load more pages a long list. The picker does not filter by publication status — you can place any of your media — and whether a placed item actually reaches readers is decided after you save, by the published-and-current rule above (the reader preview flags the difference; see below). A block with nothing chosen shows No items chosen on the board rather than an empty frame to readers.
  • Show or hide a block without removing it — Maturity: Built. Each block carries a Shown / Hidden switch. Hidden means declared but not shown to readers: the block stays on your board — with its chosen items and order intact — so you can bring it back with one click, but it is left off the public homepage entirely. This is separate from publishing individual items: hiding a block hides the whole block, while the publishing rule above governs each item within a shown block. Hiding takes effect on save, like any other board change — there is nothing to re-publish.
  • Preview the reader's view — Maturity: Built. Preview reader view renders, read-only, what your saved board would look like to a reader — in board order, with hidden blocks left out. Within a shown block it marks any item a reader cannot yet see: Not published (the item's entry is not published) or Not saved (a change you have not saved yet); if every item in a section is marked, it says the section reads empty to readers. This is your one confirmation before you rely on the arrangement — the board is live-on-save, with no later checkpoint.

What you can do today​

  • Feature your 3D models on the homepage. The 3D model showcase is the block that is fully working end to end: add it, then pick and order the models you want up front. They appear on the public homepage once their entries are published — the reader preview flags any that are not yet published. This is the recommended starting point for a live homepage.
  • Lay out the rest of the board ahead of time. Place the Scaffolded blocks (Map, Timeline, Relationship network) where you want them; they hold their spot and describe what they will draw on, and begin showing content as each is completed — no rework to your layout when that happens.
  • Stage a block privately. Add a block and choose its items while it is Hidden — it stays on your board, with its items visible to you there, but off the public homepage. Review it on the board itself while hidden; Preview reader view deliberately leaves hidden blocks out (it mirrors the public reader), so use it to confirm the block is not yet public, then switch it to Shown and preview again to see how it reads. Nothing you stage while hidden reaches the public homepage until you show it.
  • Take something down instantly. Switch a block to Hidden and save; it leaves the public homepage on the next load, and its items and order are kept for whenever you want it back.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
A block sits on your board but readers do not see it on the public homepageThe block is set to HiddenSwitch it to Shown and save. Check Preview reader view, which also leaves hidden blocks out, to confirm.
A block is Shown but appears empty to readers, or is missing items you addedThose items are not published, or the version you expected is no longer the current oneThe homepage only shows published, current content. Publish the items (or make the intended version current); arranging a block does not publish its items.
In Preview reader view, an item carries a Not published or Not saved flagNot published — the item's entry is not yet published; Not saved — you have unsaved board changesFor Not saved, save the board. For Not published, publish the item's entry (see Media Review Surface); until then a reader sees the block without that item.
A block in the picker cannot be addedIt is at the Design-ahead stage — shown so you can see what is coming, but not yet addableUse a Building now or Scaffolded block for now; the design-ahead blocks become addable as they are completed.
A block you placed offers no way to choose specific itemsIt is a Scaffolded block, which does not yet take a hand-picked item listUse the 3D model showcase (Building now) when you need to choose and order specific items; scaffolded blocks explain in place what they will draw on.
You removed a block by mistakeRemove widget takes the block off the boardAdd it again from Add a widget and re-pick its items. To take a block off readers' view without losing its items, use Hidden instead of Remove widget.
