Vocabulary Grounding

Audience: Tenant administrator. Last aligned with platform state: 2026-05-25 Platform version: V2 (pre-1.0)

Purpose​

Vocabulary entries on their own are per-environment labels. Grounding gives those labels external identity by linking each entry to an authoritative external definition — Wikidata for entities, PeriodO for historical periods. Read this when you are confirming AI-proposed groundings during onboarding, when you add a new vocabulary entry and want to anchor it externally, or when a researcher reports an incorrect grounding.

A grounded entry satisfies the safety-floor gate, makes your environment intelligible to external readers, and lets AI assistance reason about your vocabulary against a known reference.

Features​

  • Wikidata grounding lookup — propose and confirm Wikidata Q-identifiers for TenantVocabularyEntry rows. Substrate: Spec 0026 + customers/services/vocabulary_derivation.py (PR 61951ad6, 2026-05-23) · Maturity: Active · Limit: only the tenant admin role can confirm a grounding.
  • PeriodO authority lookup — propose and confirm PeriodO ARK identifiers for period entries; the grounded entry carries the temporal range and regional scope from the authority. Substrate: Spec 0026 (PR 8c7a5969, 2026-05-23) · Maturity: Active · Limit: scoped to PeriodO's regional/temporal model; periods outside the model stay ungrounded.
  • Static AI-discovery banner — fixed, platform-controlled message above the grounding workflow that frames what the AI is and is not doing; anchored to a known anchor point for external auditors. Substrate: Spec 0026 design.md "AI-discovery banner contract" · Maturity: Active · Limit: tenant admins cannot customise the banner wording.
  • Structured LLMFeedbackSignal capture — typed feedback when an AI-proposed grounding is wrong or low-quality; replaces free-form correction text with a queryable record. Substrate: Spec 0026 + epistemic/models.py LLMFeedbackSignal (PR d8696bc5, 2026-05-22) · Maturity: Active · Limit: signals are tenant-local; they improve future proposals in your environment only.
  • Grounded-entry status — the grounded status on TenantVocabularyEntry is one of three that satisfies the safety-floor gate. Substrate: ADR-044 §3 · Maturity: Active · Limit: changing a grounded entry to a different external identifier requires re-confirmation.

Usability — how you use it​

Grounding lives in the same admin surface as the vocabulary list.

  • Confirm a proposed Wikidata grounding: open the vocabulary surface, find the row marked with a proposed Wikidata match, review the candidate (the surface shows the Q-identifier and the authority's display label), click Confirm. The entry's status moves to grounded and carries the external identifier.
  • Ground a period entry against PeriodO: open the period view, find the period name, accept a proposed PeriodO match or paste a PeriodO ARK identifier directly. The grounded entry carries start year, end year, region, and the authority reference.
  • Reject a wrong AI proposal with structured feedback: open the proposal, click Reject, choose a structured reason (wrong, ambiguous, low-quality, out-of-scope). The LLMFeedbackSignal is recorded; the platform uses it to improve future proposals in your environment.
  • Verify a grounding propagated: confirmed groundings appear in tooltips, exports, and audit views, but not in researcher-facing wording — the external identifier is referenced, never substituted.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
The Wikidata picker returns no candidates for a term you know exists in WikidataThe authority lookup uses your entry's surface form; if the surface form is highly tenant-specific, the lookup may missPaste the Q-identifier directly into the grounding row, or refine the vocabulary entry's surface form to a more canonical variant and re-propose.
A PeriodO match looks approximately right but the temporal range is offPeriodO carries multiple period definitions per name; the platform picked one but it is not the one your researchers meanReject with structured feedback. Browse PeriodO for the correct ARK and paste it directly.
You see ExternalCredentialUnavailable when proposing a groundingThe authority adapter requires credentials (or the credential reference resolves to an unavailable secret)Check ExternalServiceCredential records and the configured AWS Secrets Manager reference. Wikidata and PeriodO public lookups should not require credentials; this error usually means the platform is attempting a privileged lookup path.
A grounded entry shows up as proposed again after a re-importA re-import refreshed the underlying entry without preserving the grounding linkThis should not happen; the importer's --merge-replace mode preserves non-source fields including the grounding. File an issue with the import job id and entry id.
You want to communicate a tenant-specific caveat about a groundingThe static AI-discovery banner is platform-controlled and cannot be customisedUse your environment's general announcement surface; do not work around the banner. The banner is a contract with external auditors that the platform commits to keep static.
