Efficiency Tips

Audience: Tenant administrator. Last aligned with platform state: 2026-05-25 Platform version: V2 (pre-1.0)

Purpose​

Efficiency tips are short, contextual nudges the platform surfaces to researchers — in the import wizard, in the workspace, on the dashboard — suggesting a faster way to do what the researcher is already doing. As tenant admin, you control whether tips appear in your environment and you can read aggregate acceptance ratios per tip family. Read this when deciding whether to enable tips at onboarding, when a researcher reports an unwanted nudge, or when you want to know how well tips are landing.

Tenant admins own this surface because tip wording is platform-emitted text shown to researchers — and you own how the platform speaks.

Features​

  • Client.tips_enabled toggle — environment-wide on/off; off by default for new environments. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.A + customers/models.py Client.tips_enabled (PR aee2d669, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: master toggle; off means no tips emitted regardless of any other setting.
  • tipsForContext GraphQL query — fetches tips for the current context (stage, surface, session id); the React TipsPanel consumes it. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.A + Phase 6.B (PR aee2d669 + a526dbcf, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: tips never block researcher action; researcher can dismiss any tip and proceed.
  • {% tips_for %} Django template tag — server-side render hook for wizard stages and other Django-rendered surfaces; takes a min_confidence kwarg. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.B + R2 absorption (PR c5f7a9b9, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: tag-side filtering centralised in customers/services/tip_filter.py after R4 absorption (PR 9b7d2cff).
  • Wizard Stage 3 surface — primary surfacing point; tip cards render alongside proposal cards. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.B SPA (PR 5cb9a3d8, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: tip wording passes through voice substitution; you do not edit tip text directly.
  • tip_family_acceptance_ratios read-only view — aggregate (accepted / shown) ratio per tip family for your environment. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.C + Phase 6.D (PR 03bc448f, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: k-anonymity-floor clamped — families below TIP_ROLLUP_MIN_K (R3 absorption) report a neutral value, not a precise ratio.
  • Auto-pause — platform staff may auto-pause a tip family across all environments if it is performing badly; not exposed in tenant admin. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.D + TIP_AUTO_PAUSE event type (PR f325571e, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: platform-staff side; visible only as the family ceasing to fire for your researchers. See super admin tip auto-pause.
  • tips_telemetry_enabled axis — per-environment telemetry capture axis distinct from tips_enabled. Substrate: Spec 0030 Phase 6.E (PR f325571e, 2026-05-25) · Maturity: Active · Limit: telemetry capture requires DPO sign-off captured in your environment configuration; see Telemetry.

Usability — how you use it​

The efficiency-tip surface lives inside the tenant-admin shell.

  • Enable tips for your environment: set Client.tips_enabled = True. Off by default for new environments; turn on once you have reviewed how tips will appear to your researchers.
  • Read acceptance ratios: open the tip-recommender metrics tab. The ratio is per family, computed across your researchers only, k-anonymity-floor clamped. Use this to decide whether to leave tips enabled or to flag a problematic family for the platform team.
  • Disable tips mid-session: set Client.tips_enabled = False. Researchers looking at a stage with tip cards will see those cards disappear on the next stage transition; in-flight tips do not persist.
  • Change tip wording for your environment: change the relevant TenantVocabularyEntry (the wording propagates through the voice substrate). You do not author tip text directly.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
Researchers report seeing no tips even though tips are enabledEither no tip family currently applies to the surfaces they are using, or auto-pause has paused the relevant family platform-wideConfirm Client.tips_enabled = True and check the tip-recommender metrics tab for which families fired recently. If a family you expected is missing, the platform may have auto-paused it; see tip auto-pause.
Acceptance ratio for a family shows a neutral midpoint valueSample size below TIP_ROLLUP_MIN_K — the k-anonymity floor clamps small samples rather than reporting a noisy precise ratioThis is by design (R3 absorption, 2026-05-25). Wait for more events to accumulate, or read the family across a longer time window.
A researcher reports unwanted nudge wordingTip wording resolves through the voice substrate against your TenantVocabularyEntry rowsFind the canonical key the tip uses and edit your matching TenantVocabularyEntry. The wording propagates immediately.
You enabled tips but the wizard's Stage 3 shows no tips panelThe Stage 3 template tag is filtered by min_confidence; low-confidence proposals do not surfaceThe threshold defaults to 0.6 (R2 absorption round 4, PR c5f7a9b9); confirm by inspecting the rendered template. Tips are intentionally conservative; absence is not a bug.
You see a tip family in the metrics but not in your researchers' surfacesThe auto-pause platform decision applies across environments; once paused, the family stops firing for everyoneConfirmation requires platform-team visibility; this is a one-way decision from your side.
