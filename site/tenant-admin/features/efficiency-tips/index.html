Efficiency Tips

Purpose​

Tips are short, contextual suggestions the platform shows researchers while they work — in the review step of the import wizard and in the workspace — pointing out a faster way to do what they are already doing, or a field worth filling in. Tips are advisory: they never change data and never block anyone.

As environment administrator, you decide whether tips appear in your environment at all, and the names tips use for your record types and fields come from your environment's vocabulary. Read this page when deciding whether to leave tips on at onboarding, when a researcher reports an unwanted or oddly worded suggestion, or when a kind of tip stops appearing.

Features​

  • Environment-wide tips switch — one setting turns tips on or off for your whole environment. It is on by default for new environments; off means no tips appear anywhere, regardless of any other setting. Maturity: Active.
  • Tips during import review — when a researcher makes the same correction repeatedly while reviewing an import, a tip suggests fixing the underlying rule once instead of correcting the remaining rows by hand. Tip cards appear beside the correction cards in the review step. Maturity: Active.
  • Tips in the workspace — when a selected record has noticeably fewer filled fields than similar records in your environment, a tip names a field worth adding. Maturity: Active.
  • Confidence-tiered display — the most confident suggestions appear directly beside the researcher's work; middling ones wait under a collapsed "More suggestions" heading; anything below the confidence bar is not shown at all. Maturity: Active.
  • Wording through your vocabulary — tip sentences are written by the platform, and the names they use for your record types and fields resolve through your environment's vocabulary. You adjust those names through vocabulary entries; you do not edit tip text directly. Maturity: Active.
  • Per-family acceptance figures — the platform keeps aggregate figures per kind of tip — how often that kind was shown and how researchers responded — for your environment only, over a rolling 30-day window. Individual researcher actions are not reported. Maturity: Built; a self-service view of these figures is not yet available — contact your platform operator if you need them.
  • Automatic quiet-down — a kind of tip your researchers consistently pass over can pause itself in your environment. The pause requires sustained low uptake (around four weeks) and a minimum number of researcher responses, so a quiet week does not silence a useful tip. Your platform operator is notified when a kind of tip pauses. Maturity: Built.
  • Separate measurement switch — whether tip activity is measured is a separate per-environment setting from whether tips display, so tips can be shown without their activity being measured. When tips are off, nothing is shown, so there is no tip activity to measure. Measurement records which tips appeared and how researchers responded, without recording who. Maturity: Built. See Telemetry for what your environment records.

Usability — how you use it​

  • Decide at onboarding: tips are on by default for a new environment. Review how tips will read to your researchers — the record-type and field names come from your vocabulary (see Voice and Vocabulary) — and either leave tips on or have them switched off until your vocabulary is ready.
  • Turn tips off for your environment: the environment-wide setting lives on your environment's platform record — contact your platform operator to change it. Once tips are off, no new tips are served anywhere; anything already on a researcher's screen clears the next time the page or wizard step loads.
  • Change how a tip is worded: adjust the matching vocabulary entry, and the new name appears the next time the tip is shown. If the problem is in the sentence around the names rather than the names themselves, contact your platform operator.
  • When a kind of tip goes quiet: this may be the automatic quiet-down. Re-enabling a quieted kind of tip is not self-service — contact your platform operator.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
Researchers see no tips even though tips are switched onNo tip pattern currently applies — tips fire only after repeated same-shape corrections during import review, or when a record clearly lags similar records — or a kind of tip has been quietedTips are deliberately conservative; silence usually means no pattern was found. If a kind of tip that used to appear has stopped, contact your platform operator to confirm whether it was quieted.
The import review step shows no tips panelNothing cleared the confidence bar for that sessionNothing to fix — low-confidence suggestions are held back by design, and the panel does not render when there is nothing to show.
A researcher reports an unwanted or oddly worded suggestionThe tip draws your record-type and field names from your environment's vocabulary, and the entry it found reads poorly in contextAdjust the matching vocabulary entry — see Voice and Vocabulary. If the wording problem is in the surrounding sentence, contact your platform operator.
A kind of tip stopped appearing after weeks of normal useIt may have paused automatically because researchers consistently passed it overContact your platform operator to review and re-enable it. Consider whether different vocabulary wording would help it land better.
