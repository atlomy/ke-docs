Permissions Panel

Audience: Tenant administrator. Last aligned with platform state: 2026-05-25 Platform version: V2 (pre-1.0)

Purpose​

The Permissions Panel at /admin/permissions/ is the single surface for who-can-do-what in your environment. Contributors, role composition, project rules, wording overrides, ownership transfers, and the audit trail all live here. Read this when adding or removing a contributor, composing a custom role, transferring authored content, or answering "who reviewed what, and when?".

The panel consolidates what earlier versions of the platform spread across the standard Django admin, scattered settings pages, and ad-hoc forms.

Features​

  • Contributors tab — list of contributors in your environment with their assigned role and last activity; add, remove, reassign here. Substrate: Spec 0018 Sprint C admin backend (PR 7e14d234, 2026-05-20) + customers/services/permission_admin.py · Maturity: Active · Limit: tenant-scoped; the panel cannot see contributors in other environments.
  • Roles tab + custom role composition — standard roles pre-loaded; custom roles compose from a fixed set of permission primitives. Standard roles can be renamed inline (surface label only). Substrate: Spec 0018 Sprint C role-name override (PR 467f181b, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: standard role primitives are fixed by the platform.
  • Project rules — per-project policy that further scopes a role's permissions within that project. Substrate: Spec 0018 Sprint C scope picker (PR b3ea0219, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: rules narrow, never expand; effective permission is the intersection.
  • Wording overrides — voice-substrate-backed surface-label changes scoped to permission-related strings (button labels on review actions, confirmation phrases, role names). Substrate: ADR-044 + Spec 0018 Sprint C confirmation phrases (PR b36d3d58, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: composes with the global voice substrate; no separate translation table to maintain.
  • Audit tab — chronological audit trail of permission changes, ownership transfers, vocabulary edits, AI-reviewed commits. Substrate: Spec 0018 Sprint C audit tab (PR 63c31ac9, 2026-05-20) + receipt audit records (PR 28b33c65, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: append-only; corrections are appended, the original entry stays.
  • Ownership-transfer modal — reassigns a contributor's authored content to another contributor with explicit confirmation; records a transfer event. Substrate: Spec 0018 Sprint C (PR b3ea0219, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: non-destructive at the row level; the original author's contribution history remains visible.
  • Vocabulary startup linter — read-only lint pass on panel load; surfaces missing entries, broken grounding references, inconsistencies. Substrate: Spec 0018 C.1.14 (PR 9672e561, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: never fixes anything; acting on findings is your decision.
  • Receipt audit records — structured view of completed apply operations including post-apply outcome and deferred follow-ups. Substrate: Spec 0018 C.8 (PR 28b33c65, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: indexed for quick lookup; structured reason codes are platform-defined.
  • DPIA operations log — data-protection-impact-assessment trail for permission changes affecting personal-data fields. Substrate: Spec 0018 C.1.15 DPIA ops log (PR 0f37856a, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: always-on; cannot be disabled.
  • Recommender metrics tab — review-learning capture controls; toggles whether the platform captures reviewer decisions as LLMFeedbackSignal records to improve future AI proposals. Substrate: Spec 0018 C.6 (PR 2fea44b3, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: off by default; opt-in per environment; capture is structured, not free-text.
  • Permissions panel density control — visual density toggle persisted per contributor. Substrate: Spec 0018 (PR ae829c97, 2026-05-21) · Maturity: Active · Limit: cosmetic only; affects rendering, not permissions.
  • Operations incident timeline — visible-to-admin view of recent incidents affecting your environment. Substrate: Spec 0018 C.7.3 (PR 52459371, 2026-05-20) · Maturity: Active · Limit: tenant-local view; the cross-environment incident surface lives in super admin incident operations.

Usability — how you use it​

The canonical URL is /admin/permissions/. The panel is tab-organised; the structural tabs are the ones above (exact labels follow your environment's voice configuration).

  • Add a contributor: Contributors tab, add form, choose role. The role must be assigned before the contributor can do anything.
  • Compose a custom role: Roles tab, compose from the available permission primitives. Rename inline via the edit-pencil control if you want a different surface label; the underlying primitives are fixed.
  • Apply a project rule: Project rules tab. The rule scope picker lets you target the rule to specific projects or archetypes.
  • Transfer authored content: open a contributor row, use the ownership-transfer modal. You walk through reassigning their authored content; explicit confirmation required; transfer event recorded in audit.
  • Read the audit trail: Audit tab. Each row carries actor, timestamp, target, structured reason code. Receipt audit records add the post-apply outcome for completed operations.
  • Enable review-learning capture: Recommender metrics tab. Off by default; flip the toggle to enable; capture goes through LLMFeedbackSignal. The decision is logged in the audit tab.

After you change a permission or rule, a post-apply edit pip appears next to the affected row for the rest of your session so you do not lose track of in-flight changes.

Troubleshooting​

The panel shows a vocabulary startup-linter banner with findingsThe lint pass detected missing entries, broken grounding references, or inconsistenciesClick into the surfaced rows. The lint is read-only; you decide whether to add, ground, or remove entries. See Voice and Vocabulary.
Renaming a standard role did not change the role's effective permissionsRenaming is a surface-label override; the underlying permission primitives of standard roles are fixedThis is the correct behaviour. Compose a custom role if you need different primitives.
A contributor's effective permission is narrower than their role suggestsA project rule narrowed it for that specific projectOpen Project rules. Effective permission is the intersection of role and project rule.
You need to correct an erroneous audit rowThe audit trail is append-onlyAppend a correction. The original entry stays; the correction is its own audit row.
The ownership-transfer modal will not let you complete a transferThe destination contributor lacks a role, or the source contributor has authored content in a project whose rule blocks the transferAssign the destination contributor a suitable role first, or check the project rule that is blocking.
Recommender metrics capture is enabled but the AI proposals do not seem to improveCapture is structured but limited in volume; signals accumulate over timeThis is expected early on. The capture is tenant-local; signals improve future proposals in your environment only.
