Create, rename, and delete a collection

Purpose​

A collection is one of the kinds of things you keep track of in your workspace — people, manuscripts, terms, events, and so on. You can create a new collection, rename one, and remove one, all from the workspace sidebar, without leaving for an administration screen. This page covers those three actions and what each one does to your records.

Your records are never deleted by anything on this page. Removing a collection only removes it from your sidebar — the records underneath stay.

Create a new collection​

  1. Open the workspace.
  2. In the sidebar, select Add table.
  3. Give the collection a name in Table name — for example, "Glossary", "Sources", or "People".
  4. Choose a Table meaning — what each row in this collection represents. The choices are:
    • Terms & concepts — a glossary or controlled vocabulary.
    • Documents & texts — works or sources you cite or read as a whole.
    • Named things — people, places, organizations, or other named things.
    • Events — things that happened in time.
    • Passages — excerpts or quoted spans drawn from documents.
    • Annotations & notes — notes or comments attached to other records.
    • Media — images, audio, video, or 3D models.
  5. Optionally pick a Sidebar icon.
  6. Select Create table.

The collection meaning sets how the platform stores and connects the records, so choose the one that best fits what you are recording. A new collection starts empty and with no project-specific fields; you add fields and rows next (see Build a knowledge environment from scratch without importing).

"3D" is a property of an individual media item, not a separate kind of collection. Choose Media for images, audio, video, and 3D models alike.

Register a collection the platform built for you​

When you import data, the platform shows your records grouped into collections automatically. A collection that came from your data this way can be registered — turned into a collection you can rename, configure, and delete — while keeping all of its records exactly as they are.

  1. In the sidebar, open the actions menu for the collection (right-click it, or use its actions control).
  2. Select Register this table….
  3. Confirm the name, adjust the icon if you wish, and create it.

Registering does not change or move your records; it gives the collection a permanent home in your sidebar so its name, icon, and settings are yours to manage. A collection that holds no records yet cannot be registered — start one from scratch with Add table instead.

Rename a collection​

  1. In the sidebar, open the actions menu for the collection.
  2. Select Rename table….
  3. Change the Table name or the Sidebar icon.
  4. Select Save changes.

Renaming changes only how the collection appears in your sidebar. It does not touch the records inside it.

Remove a collection​

  1. In the sidebar, open the actions menu for the collection.
  2. Select Delete table….
  3. Read the confirmation, then select Delete table.

Removing a collection unregisters it — it takes the collection out of your sidebar but never deletes the records underneath. If the collection still holds records, it remains browsable as an automatic collection built from your data.

If the collection has published entries, its curated publish settings are reset, and the next time you publish it falls back to defaults. Pages and citations you already published stay exactly as they are.

Who can do this​

Creating, renaming, and removing collections is available to editors and administrators. If these actions appear dimmed in your sidebar, your role does not include them — the sidebar shows the reason in place. Ask your environment administrator if you need access.
