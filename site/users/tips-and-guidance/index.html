Tips and Guidance

Purpose​

While you work, the platform may show small tips beside the record or the import you are reviewing — short notes about a faster way to do what you are already doing, or about something a record might be missing. Tips are advice, not commands. They never change your data, never block you, and go away on their own once they no longer apply. Read this page if you are wondering why a tip appeared, what to do with it, or why one you expected did not.

Features​

  • Improvement tips in the workspace — when the record you have selected has noticeably fewer filled fields than similar records in your environment, a tip names a field you could add. The comparison needs at least three similar records, and the tip stays silent when there is nothing meaningful to suggest. Maturity: Active.
  • Efficiency tips during import review — when you make the same correction three or more times while reviewing an import, a tip notes the pattern, so you can adjust the rule once instead of correcting every remaining row by hand. Maturity: Active.
  • A panel that stays out of the way — tips appear in a panel beside your work. In the workspace, the most confident tips show directly and the rest wait under a collapsed "More suggestions" heading; during import review, only confident tips appear. When nothing clears the confidence bar, no panel appears at all. Maturity: Active.
  • Environment-wide off switch — your environment administrator can turn tips off for the whole environment; when off, no tips appear anywhere. Maturity: Active.
  • Standardization tips — a third kind, which recognizes when a value matches a term already in your environment's controlled vocabulary, is built but not yet shown anywhere in the product. Maturity: Built — not yet available.
  • Quiet-down for unhelpful tips — the platform can pause a kind of tip that researchers in an environment consistently pass over, so persistent noise switches itself off. This automatic pause is built but not yet running. Maturity: Built — not yet active.
  • Aggregate-only usage figures — tip measurement, once running, records tip activity without naming the researcher, and figures about whether tips help are reported only in aggregate, withheld until enough activity contributes. Measurement is built but not yet collecting. Maturity: Built — not yet active.

Usability — how you use it​

When a tip appears​

You are editing a record or reviewing an import. A short note appears beside your work: one sentence of advice, with a brief reason underneath. Decide:

  • Act on it. Make the edit it points to, the way you normally would. A tip is text — it carries no button and runs nothing on your behalf. Every change remains an edit you make yourself.
  • Pass. Keep working. Nothing counts against you, and the tip goes away once it no longer applies — when the field is filled, or the review session moves on.

That is the rhythm: small notes beside your work, yours to act on or pass over, never blocking.

A worked example​

You are editing a person record for Galen. Similar person records in your environment carry more detail on average, and this one has an empty birth-place field. An improvement tip surfaces, pointing out that records like this one usually have more fields filled and naming the birth place as a place to start. If the omission is deliberate — the birth place is genuinely unknown, or out of scope for your work — keep going. The tip does not insist, and it disappears once the comparison no longer holds.

Later, you are reviewing an import and correct the same field the same way on a third row. An efficiency tip appears, noting that you have made the same correction several times. That is your cue to consider fixing the underlying mapping rule for the remaining rows instead of repeating the correction one row at a time.

During import review​

When the review step of the import wizard is open, the tip panel sits beside the correction cards. Tips there fire only after a clear pattern — several corrections of the same shape within the session — and only when the platform is confident the suggestion is worth your attention. A blank panel during review is normal silence, not a fault.

What tips never do​

Tips never change your data — there is no action a tip performs for you, so nothing happens without your own edit. Tips never penalize you for passing them over; ignored tips do not accumulate into a score or a warning. And tips never substitute for configuration — if your environment's required fields, vocabularies, or workflows are out of date, the right fix is to update the configuration, not to lean on tips. Raise that with your environment administrator.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
A tip you expected never appears.The trigger was not met — too few similar records to compare against, no repeated correction pattern, or the suggestion was not confident enough to show. Tips may also be turned off for your environment.Confirm the trigger applies; an improvement tip needs at least three similar records in your environment. If tips seem off entirely, contact your environment administrator.
The tip panel during import review is blank.No tip cleared the confidence bar for this session, or you have not yet repeated a correction enough times for a pattern to register.Nothing to fix — silence is intentional.
A whole kind of tip stopped appearing.Tips were turned off for your environment, or that kind's trigger no longer applies to your records.If tips are gone everywhere, contact your environment administrator; otherwise there is nothing to fix.
Tips feel noisy or distracting.The patterns in your data are triggering more suggestions than is helpful to you.Contact your environment administrator — tips can be turned off for the whole environment.
