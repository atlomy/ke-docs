Make a field required

When a field is required, a record cannot be saved while that field is empty. The platform will not let you activate the draft until you fill it in.

Setting a field as required is always your decision. Your import never sets this automatically — the platform writes "not required" at creation and leaves the choice to you.

Steps​

  1. Open the collection whose field you want to change.
  2. Click Field Definitions in the toolbar or sidebar of that collection. (This opens the field configuration view for the collection — not the individual record.)
  3. Find the field in the list. Click its row to open its settings.
  4. Turn on Required. Save.

From now on, any new record in that collection will require a value in this field before it can be activated. Existing records already in the collection are not retroactively blocked.

What "required" means at the record level​

When you create a new record and blur out of a required field while it is still empty, the form surfaces a validation hint beneath that field. The Activate button stays greyed out until the required field has a value. You can save the draft with the field empty — autosave keeps your progress — but activation is blocked.

Notes​

  • Required applies to new records you create. It does not affect records already in the collection.
  • Your import never overrides this setting. If you mark a field required, re-importing your source leaves your setting intact (see Rename a field label for more on which settings survive re-imports).
  • Removing the required flag works the same way: open Field Definitions, find the field, turn Required off, and save.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
The Required toggle is not visible in Field Definitions.Your role does not have permission to change field configuration.Ask a project administrator to make the change.
An existing record shows no required-field hint even though the field is empty.Required applies only to records created after the setting was turned on.Existing records are not retroactively blocked.
The Activate button is still grey after filling in the required field.Another required field may also be empty.Check for other red-outlined or hint-bearing fields on the form.
