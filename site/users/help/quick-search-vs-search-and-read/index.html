Choose between the quick search bar and Search & Read

Your workspace has two search tools. They are intentionally separate — the right one depends on whether you want to jump straight to a record you already know, or browse, filter, and read across many records in one place.

Quick search — for jumping to a known record​

Open quick search from the Search database… control at the top of the workspace, or press Ctrl+Shift+F (⌘⇧F on Mac). A search panel opens over whatever you are doing.

Type a word. Results appear grouped by collection, each one showing the record's label and the fields where your term matched. Select a result and the platform opens that record directly — whichever collection it lives in.

The panel also controls the scope: search everything you have access to, search only the current table, or narrow the search to a single collection.

Use quick search when:

  • You want to land on one specific record and open it.
  • You know roughly what you are looking for.
  • You are in the middle of editing and need to jump to a related record fast.

Search & Read — for browsing, comparing, and reading in place​

Search & Read is a full search page you can dwell in. Open it from the quick search panel — the Open full Search & Read button at the bottom carries your typed term over — or from the command palette (Ctrl+K / ⌘K), where it appears as Search & Read.

The result list is built for reading. For any result you can expand its content inline, open a preview panel beside the list, or click through to the record itself. You can page through the results and sort them by relevance, by recently modified, or by label.

Filtering goes further than quick search. Switch to Advanced mode to:

  • Scope the search to one collection, or leave it across everything you can access. Each collection carries a type badge, so you can see at a glance what kind of records it holds.
  • Add conditions on field values. Each condition names a field, a match type (contains, equals, or starts with), and a value; a condition can also be negated. Conditions combine with Match all or with any.

The page address encodes your query, filters, page, and sort order — paste the address to a colleague and they see the same results. See Share a search.

Use Search & Read when:

  • You want to look at several records, not only one.
  • You want to apply filters — by collection, by a field value, by any combination — and see everything that matches.
  • You want to share the search with a colleague.
  • You want to read records without navigating away from the results.

Escalating from quick search​

If quick search finds results but you want to explore the full set, or you want to narrow it with filters: select Open full Search & Read at the bottom of the quick search panel. Your search term carries over. Switch to Advanced mode to add field-level conditions.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
Quick search returns no results even though you know the record exists.Search matches against the fields your environment includes in its search configuration; your term may appear only in a field that is not covered.Try a word from a different field of the record. If it still does not appear, contact your environment administrator to review which fields are searchable.
You cannot find Search & Read.It opens from the quick search panel or the command palette — there is no permanent menu entry. Your role may also not include access to workspace search.Open quick search and select Open full Search & Read. If the page reports an error or shows no collections, contact your environment administrator.
Results show strings of letters and numbers instead of names.The collection has no display field configured, so results fall back to an internal identifier.Contact your environment administrator and ask them to set a display field for that collection.
