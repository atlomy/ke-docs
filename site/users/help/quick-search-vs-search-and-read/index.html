Choose between the quick search bar and Search & Read

The platform has two search tools that serve different needs. They are intentionally separate — the right one depends on whether you want to jump directly to a record you know exists, or browse and read across many records in one place.

The quick search bar — for jumping to a known record​

The quick search bar is the small search control in the top of the workspace. Type a word and pick a result; the platform opens that record directly.

Use quick search when:

  • You want to land on one specific record and open it.
  • You know roughly what you are looking for.
  • You are in the middle of editing and need to jump to a related record fast.

Quick search returns results across all the collections you have access to, grouped by collection name. Selecting a result takes you straight to that record's detail view.

Search & Read — for browsing, comparing, and reading in place​

Search & Read is a dedicated section of the workspace, accessible from the main navigation. It gives you a result grid you can dwell in: read multiple items without leaving the search, expand a record's full content inline, open a preview panel alongside the list, and page through hundreds of results.

Use Search & Read when:

  • You want to look at several records, not only one.
  • You want to apply filters — by collection, by a field value, by any combination — and see everything that matches.
  • You want to share the search with a colleague (the URL encodes your query and filters — see Share a search).
  • You want to read records without navigating away from the results.

Search & Read also has an advanced mode where you can combine multiple filter conditions with AND/OR logic.

What it doesJump to one recordBrowse many records in place

Escalating from quick search​

If quick search returns results but you want to explore the full set, or if you want to apply filters to narrow it: open Search & Read from the navigation, type the same term in the search box there, and switch to advanced mode to add field-level filters.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFix
Quick search returns no results even though you know the record exists.The record's collection may not have a display label configured, or the field you searched is not marked as searchable.Try Search & Read and look for the record by collection; if you find it there, ask your administrator to check the searchable-field configuration.
Search & Read is not in the navigation.The feature may not be enabled for your project yet, or your role may not have access.Ask your project administrator.
Results in Search & Read show unclear labels (e.g., strings of letters and numbers instead of real names).The collection's display label is not configured.Ask your administrator to check Field Definitions for that collection and set a display field.
