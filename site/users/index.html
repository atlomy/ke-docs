Researcher Guide

Purpose​

You are a scholar, student, archivist, or editor working in a knowledge environment built on this platform. You sign in, read, search, edit drafts, and cite records. You do not run the system; you use it. This guide is the entry point for everything researcher-facing. Read it when you join a new environment, when a control is not behaving as you expect, or when you want to know what the platform will and will not do for you.

If you configure the environment — turning columns on or off, choosing which fields are searchable, managing other researchers' permissions — see the environment administration guide instead.

Features​

The researcher surface gives you eight capabilities, ordered by how often you will reach for them.

  • Workspace table view — the main table of records for each record type, with a toolbar, a Manage columns panel, filters, a row-density toggle, cell and selected-row clipboard actions, and a Details panel for reading a full record. Maturity: Active. Limit: which controls appear for each record type is configured per environment; if a control you need is missing, your environment administrator can expose it.
  • Inline editing and drafts — edit a field directly in the table: double-click a cell to open it and press Enter to save, while longer and multilingual fields open an editor with its own Save and Cancel buttons. Date fields use a date picker; text fields that merely look like timestamps stay text until their type is changed or converted. + New row opens a drawer where your draft is saved as you work and becomes a full row when you choose Activate row. Maturity: Active. Limit: on a brand-new row, fields that link to other records become editable after the first save creates the draft.
  • Search across scripts — a quick search finds records across the collections you can access, a full Search & Read page filters and reads results in place, and the toolbar box narrows the rows already in front of you. Search matches the scripts your records carry — original script, transliteration, and translation — for fields marked searchable. Maturity: Active. Limit: only fields your environment marks searchable are covered.
  • Media and citations — records can carry images, audio, video, and 3D models, and each media item carries a citation in your project's citation style, ready to copy. Maturity: Active. Limit: which media a record carries depends on what your project attached.
  • Public reader and draft preview — a stable read-only view of published records, with an in-workspace preview that shows a record as a reader outside the project would see it. Maturity: Active. Limit: which records reach the reader depends on your project's review and publish workflow.
  • Tips and guidance — small suggestions that appear in the workspace and in multi-step wizards. You can dismiss a single tip or mute a whole family of tips. A tip only suggests; acting on it is always your choice. Maturity: Active.
  • Accessibility — keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, the row-density toggle, and controls for font size, line height, font family, and color presets. Maturity: Active. Limit: screen-reader announcement coverage continues to improve.
  • Privacy — the platform records usage as aggregate counts and timings, not transcripts of what you typed, and masks sensitive text before anything is stored. Privacy and safety explains exactly what is recorded and what is not. Maturity: Active.

Usability — how you use it​

Start with whichever matches what you want to do.

  • New to the workspace, or unsure where a control lives → Workspace walkthrough.
  • Creating a new record, picking up an abandoned draft, or editing an existing record → Adding and editing rows.
  • Hunting for a record across scripts, opening media, or reading citations → Searching and reading.
  • Curious about the small suggestions appearing beside the form or the table → Tips and guidance.
  • Driving the platform from the keyboard, or adjusting display settings → Accessibility.
  • Wondering what the platform records about you and what it does not → Privacy and safety.
  • Wanting to know what a reader outside the project sees, and how to preview it → Public reader.
  • Stuck on one specific task, or after a quick answer in the moment → the in-product Help page (/app/workspace/help) groups short task-shaped guides by topic, and a ? beside a control opens the matching guide in a drawer. Browse the same guides in the help topics (see find help on a topic).

A few conventions across all walkthroughs:

  • Bold marks an on-screen control name — for example, + New row, Add filter, Activate row.
  • A code-style font marks a path, URL fragment, or literal value.
  • Examples use abstract content ("historical figures", "manuscript editions") rather than your specific project's records. The mechanics are the same regardless.

Troubleshooting​

SymptomLikely causeFixWhere to escalate
A column you expected is missing from the table.Either hidden in the current view, or not exposed in your environment's configuration.Open Manage columns in the toolbar; if the column is not listed there, ask your environment administrator to expose it.Your environment administrator.
A field is read-only when you expected to edit it.Permissions on your account, or a workflow rule for that record type.Confirm your role with your environment administrator; if the restriction is intended, request edit permission for that record type.Your environment administrator.
A draft will not activate.A required field is still empty.The drawer highlights the required fields that are still empty — fill them, then choose Activate row again. See Adding and editing rows.Your environment administrator, if the message is unclear.
Search returns nothing for a record you know exists.The relevant field is not marked searchable in your environment's configuration.Open the record directly through its Details panel; ask your environment administrator to mark the field searchable.Your environment administrator.
